Research

At Emory, I work with Dr. Lars Ruthotto on a variety of computational problems, especially in reinforcement learning and optimal control. I also have interests in computer science education, particularly in personalized learning. As such, I also work with Dr. Davide Fossati in technology enhanced learning.

Some representative projects include:
  • Reinforcement Learning and Optimal Control
    [summer 2020 - present] Implementing RL strategies such as PPO to solve control problems. Building on insights from Berteskas' RL and OC book. Applying techniques to computatioanl neuroscience problems such as using control algorithms in treatment procedures for neurological disorders.

  • Towards Mastery Learning in Computer Science Education
    [summer 2020 - present] Constructing an unlimited (or very large) supply of practice problems, annotated by skill, that can be used in an asynchronous learning setting to help students attain "mastery" of topics at their own pace as an alternative to synchronous learning. Proficiency is determined and updated in real time by stochastic models.

  • UPenn and Mayo Clinic's Seizure Detection Challenge
    [spring 2020] Working with Yazhuo and Ramraj, developed computational algorithms that detect seizures in intracranial EEG recordings of 8 human patients with epilepsy and 4 canines with naturally-occurring epilepsy. Part of the UPenn and Mayo Clinic's Seizure Detection Challenge on Kaggle.

  • OpenAI BipedalWalker-v2 Challenge
    [fall 2019] Working with Ramraj, implemented RL strategies to train a 2D bipedal walker to navigate rough terrain. We developed: (1) Deep Deterministic Policy Gradients (DDPG); (2) Evolution Strategies (ES); and (3) Augmented Random Search (ARS) algorithms.

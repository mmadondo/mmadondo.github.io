Research

At Emory, I work with Dr. Lars Ruthotto on a variety of computational problems, especially in reinforcement learning and optimal control. I also have interests in computer science education, particularly in personalized learning. As such, I have done some work with Dr. Davide Fossati in technology enhanced learning.

Some representative projects include:
  • Reinforcement Learning and Optimal Control
    [summer 2020 - present] Formulating mean field games and control problems from a reinforcement learning (RL) perspective. Studying RL-based approaches to solving adaptive optimal control problems, particularly in systems with nonlinear, unknown, and stochastic dynamics. I am building on insights from Berteskas' RL and OC book and applying these techniques to computational neuroscience problems such as using control algorithms in treatment procedures for neurological disorders.

  • Reinforcement Learning and Graph Generation
    [fall 2020 - present] Applying model-based Reinforcement Learning strategies in the field of graph generation, in particular, to generative models that can learn to create novel graphs efficiently.

  • Towards Mastery Learning in Computer Science Education
    [May - December 2020] Constructing an unlimited (or very large) supply of practice problems, annotated by skill, that can be used in an asynchronous learning setting to help students attain "mastery" of topics at their own pace as an alternative to synchronous learning. Proficiency is determined and updated in real time by stochastic models.

  • UPenn and Mayo Clinic's Seizure Detection Challenge
    [spring 2020] Working with Yazhuo and Ramraj, developed computational algorithms that detect seizures in intracranial EEG recordings of 8 human patients with epilepsy and 4 canines with naturally-occurring epilepsy. Part of the UPenn and Mayo Clinic's Seizure Detection Challenge on Kaggle.

  • OpenAI BipedalWalker-v2 Challenge
    [fall 2019] Working with Ramraj, implemented RL strategies to train a 2D bipedal walker to navigate rough terrain. We developed: (1) Deep Deterministic Policy Gradients (DDPG); (2) Evolution Strategies (ES); and (3) Augmented Random Search (ARS) algorithms.

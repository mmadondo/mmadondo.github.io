About

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Surgery at Duke University, in the Kamaleswaran Lab. I work on oversight and control of multi-agent clinical AI, offline decision-making, and resource allocation in critical care.

Before Duke, I was a Research Scientist at the University of Chicago, developing patient-specific deep reinforcement learning for adaptive proton therapy. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Emory University, advised by Lars Ruthotto and supported by a Google PhD Fellowship, and studied Computer Science and Mathematics at The College of St. Scholastica.

Research

As clinical AI moves from models that predict to agents that act and adapt, I treat oversight as a control problem: specify the limits care must respect, then enforce them through a supervisory layer outside the learned model.
